The problem

Saved tabs shouldn't vanish.

If you keep dozens of tabs for research, work, or "read later," you may have run into one of these:

⟳

Gone after an update

You open your tab manager after an extension or browser update and your saved lists are empty.

⚡

Lost in a crash

Chrome crashes or the computer restarts, and weeks of carefully kept tabs don't come back.

☁

Sync glitches

A sync conflict or browser reset quietly overwrites your sessions with an older or empty copy.

▢

The wrong window

"Restore" brings back a different window than the one you needed — or only part of it.

What we're building

Planned features

Session Vault isn't available yet. Here's what we're planning for the first version — honest goals, not promises set in stone.

⏱

Automatic snapshots Planned

Your open windows and tabs are saved periodically in the background — no button to remember.

🔒

Local-first storage Planned

Backups live on your own device by default, not on someone else's server.

🕘

Version history Planned

Browse earlier snapshots and restore any of them, so one bad save can't overwrite everything.

⬇

One-click export Planned

Download your sessions as a JSON or HTML file that you own and can keep anywhere.

⇄

Import from tab managers Planned

Bring in lists you've already saved in other tab managers, so you don't start from scratch.
