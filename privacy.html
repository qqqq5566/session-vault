Waitlist privacy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Session Vault is a Chrome extension I'm building as an independent developer. It isn't available yet. This site only runs a waitlist. Here's exactly what happens when you join it.

What I collect

  • Your email address, which you type into the form.
  • The page URL the signup came from (for example, this site's address), which the form sends along automatically.

That's it. There are no analytics or tracking scripts on this site, and no cookies are set by me.

Who processes it

The waitlist form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form service. When you submit the form, your email and the page URL are sent to FormSubmit, which forwards the submission to me by email. The email-removal form below works the same way. FormSubmit's own handling of data is described on its website.

How I use it

I only use your email to notify you about the Session Vault beta and launch. I never sell it.

Deleting your email

To have your email removed, use the form below or reply to any email I send you later. Your request comes privately to me and isn't posted anywhere. I'll delete your address and confirm by email. Please don't post your email address in a GitHub issue, because issues are public.
